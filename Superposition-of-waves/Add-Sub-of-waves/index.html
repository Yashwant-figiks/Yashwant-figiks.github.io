🌊 Super Position of Waves

⬅ LEFT hand jerk (pulse moves →)

RIGHT hand jerk ➡ (pulse moves ←)

Press a button on each side to send pulses toward each other.
How it works: The string is made of one single material, so both pulses travel at the same speed. Where the pulses meet, the string's displacement is the sum of the two (principle of superposition).
Crest + Crest (or Trough + Trough) → they add up to a pulse of double height (constructive).
Crest + Trough → they cancel, the string becomes flat for an instant (destructive), then each pulse carries on moving unchanged.
