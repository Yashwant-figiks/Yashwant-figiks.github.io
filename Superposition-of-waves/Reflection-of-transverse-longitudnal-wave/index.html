How it works: The left string sends a single crest pulse toward the boundary. Since the right side is denser, the reflected pulse inverts (crest becomes trough), while a smaller pulse transmits through.

Tip: Toggle Wall for a fixed end — the pulse bounces back completely inverted as a trough. Toggle Ring & Rod for a free end — a light ring sliding on a frictionless rod — and the pulse bounces back upright, no inversion at all!
Longitudinal Wave: Particles oscillate parallel to wave direction. C = Compression (particles bunched)   R = Rarefaction (particles spread).

Rigid Wall mode: Compression reflects as Compression (no phase change). Click the button to switch to Non-Rigid and see inversion — Compression becomes Rarefaction!
